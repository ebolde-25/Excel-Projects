Goal of this project was to show how Excel can be used to turn raw data into actionable insights. This is a hands-on demonstration of my skill in data handling, analysis and visualisation.  
____Key Content____: Open up the file and you will find a variety of worksheets that walk through data cleaning, key statistics, pivot tables, and interactive dashboards.

## Insights:
1. The total number of games is **5,145**   
2. Top Performing Games: "Grand Theft Auto V" is clearly the top-selling game, with significantly higher sales (158.25) than the next two, "Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3" (99.09) and "Call of Duty: Black Ops II" (33.07).
3. Top Platform Sales Across Regions:
> - **Activision** has the highest sum of sales across all regions, with a large portion coming from NA Sales (North America)
> - **Electronic Arts** also shows strong global sales, with a considerable sum from NA Sales and EU Sales (Europe)
> - **Nintendo** has a good distribution across NA, EU, and JP (Japan) sales
> - **Take-Two** Interactive and Ubisoft show smaller global sales compared to the top three, but with varying regional distributions   
**4.** Regional Sales Distribution: The **Total Regional Sales ($)** pie chart indicates that NA Sales (1133) represent the largest portion of regional sales, followed by EU Sales (879) and JP Sales (209). Other sales are a smaller slice (299). This suggests North America is the primary market by sales volume shown   
5. Global Sales by Genre:
> - **Action** is by far the highest-selling genre with 673 units.
> - **Sports** (125) and **Shooter** (71) are also significant genres.
> - **Role-Playing** (463) also stands out as a strong performing genre.
> - Other genres like **Fighting**, **Misc**, **Platform**, **Puzzle**, and **Racing** have comparatively lower global sales.
6. Global Sales Trends (2010-2022): Global sales show a clear declining trend from 2010 to 2020, dropping significantly from 600.5 in 2010 to 0.3 in 2020. This indicates a substantial decrease in overall video game sales over this period, at least for the data included in this dashboard. Sales peaked around 2010-2011 (600.5 and 516.0 respectively) and steadily decreased thereafter. Then there's a slight uptick in 2017 (38.8) from 2016 (33.5), but the overall trend remains downward.
7. Filter Options: The dashboard includes filters for "Genre" and "Year," allowing me to drill down into specific categories and time periods for more detailed insights. This interactive capability allows for deeper analysis beyond the aggregated views.
